Connect Gale to your browser

Takes about a minute. Works in Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge.

  1. Open a new tab and go to chrome://extensions (Chrome) or edge://extensions (Edge).
  2. Turn on Developer mode (top-right in Chrome, left side in Edge).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose this folder:
    browser-extension (next to this page)
  4. Pin the Gale icon to the toolbar. Its popup should say Connected while Gale is running.

Keep the folder where it is: the browser loads the extension from it. Uninstalling Gale removes it.
