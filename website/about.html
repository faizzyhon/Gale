About Gale

Gale is a free download manager for Windows, made for people who want fast, reliable downloads without paying for a licence or putting up with ads.

Many download managers for Windows are paid or come with ads. Gale was started as a free alternative that anyone can use, read and improve. It does the things people rely on a download manager for: faster downloads over several connections, resuming after interruptions, taking over browser downloads, saving videos, and running queues on a schedule.

Gale is built with Rust for the download engine, Tauri for the Windows app, and React for the interface. Video downloads use the open-source tools yt-dlp and FFmpeg.
