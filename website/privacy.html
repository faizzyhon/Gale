Privacy policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026 Applies to: the Gale Download Manager app for Windows, Linux and macOS and the Gale browser extension for Chrome and Edge Developer: Muhammad Faizan — faizzyhon.com — faizzyhon@gmail.com

Summary

Gale works entirely on your computer. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no telemetry. The developer does not run any server for Gale and does not receive, collect, sell or share any of your data.

What the app stores on your computer

To work, Gale keeps the following in a local database and settings file in your user profile (Windows: %APPDATA%\com.faizzyhon.gale; Linux: ~/.local/share/com.faizzyhon.gale; macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/com.faizzyhon.gale):

  • your download list and history: web address, file name, size, save folder, status, dates
  • your settings, categories, queues, schedules and site rules
  • a short log of what the browser extension sent to Gale (shown in Settings → Browser)
  • in-app notifications

Diagnostic logs are written to %LOCALAPPDATA%\com.faizzyhon.gale\logs. They contain download ids, error codes and error messages only, never cookies or request headers.

If you set a proxy password, it is saved in the system keychain (Windows Credential Manager, the macOS Keychain, or the Secret Service on Linux, such as GNOME Keyring or KWallet), not in the database or settings file.

None of this leaves your computer unless you copy it yourself (for example with Settings → Advanced → Back up).

What the browser extension does with your data

The extension only talks to the Gale app on the same computer, through Chrome's native messaging. It does not send anything to the developer or to any other server.

  • Downloads you start: the address, file name, size, type and referring page of downloads that match your interception rules are passed to Gale so Gale can download them.
  • Cookies: if "Send the site's cookies with downloads" is on (it is by default), the cookies for that one download address are passed to Gale so downloads from sites you are signed in to work. Gale holds them in memory for that download only; they are never written to disk or logs. You can turn this off in the extension options.
  • Media on pages: to show the Download button on videos, the extension notes the addresses of video and audio streams a page loads. This list stays in the extension's memory for that tab and is cleared when the tab closes.
  • Video qualities: so the quality menu opens without waiting, the extension passes the address of a video page to Gale when the page opens on a video site (such as YouTube or Vimeo), or when you point at a streamed video on another site. Gale then reads the available qualities from that site, the same site your browser is already showing. The answer is kept in the extension's session memory for 15 minutes and cleared when the browser closes.
  • Magnet links: when you click a magnet link, its address and the page address are passed to Gale.
  • Links you choose: "Download all links" and "Download selected links" read the links on the current page only when you pick that menu item.
  • Settings: the extension's own settings are saved with the browser's storage. If you use Chrome Sync, Chrome syncs them with your Google account like any other extension setting. The Firefox version works the same way and does not collect data (it declares this to Firefox as "no data collected").

Network connections

Gale connects only to:

  • the servers you download files from (including video sites, when you download a video or the extension asks for a video's qualities)
  • for torrents: the torrent's trackers, other BitTorrent peers and the BitTorrent DHT network. Peers see your IP address and which torrent you are downloading, and receive pieces of it from Gale while it downloads; this is how BitTorrent works. Gale stops sharing when the download finishes or is paused
  • your proxy server, if you set one
  • GitHub, when you click "Update yt-dlp" in Settings (on Linux and macOS also the first time a video is downloaded), to fetch the latest yt-dlp release
  • GitHub, once each time Gale starts and when you click "Check for updates", to read the version number of the latest Gale release (api.github.com/repos/faizzyhon/Gale/releases/latest). No information about you or your downloads is sent; GitHub sees your IP address like any website. Turn this off in Settings → General ("Tell me when a new version of Gale is available")
  • Hugging Face (huggingface.co, or hf-mirror.com if you choose the mirror), only when you use the AI Models page: your search words or the model name. If you saved an access token, it is sent to Hugging Face with those requests and nowhere else; the token is kept in the system keychain, not in Gale's files
  • SponsorBlock (sponsor.ajay.app), only if you turn on "Remove sponsor segments": the id of the YouTube video you download, to get its sponsor segments

If you choose several networks in Settings → Network, Gale opens connections to the download server through each of them; the server then sees each network's IP address.

When a download is redirected to a different site, Gale does not send that site the cookies or sign-in headers that belong to the first one.

Clipboard: with "Watch the clipboard for download links" on (Settings → General; off by default), Gale reads the text on the clipboard about once a second while it runs, to notice copied download links. The text is not stored or sent anywhere. With the setting off, Gale does not read the clipboard.

Gale does not contact the developer.

Gale for Android

The Android app follows the same rules: no account, no analytics, no ads, nothing sent to the developer.

  • The download list and settings are stored in the app's private storage; finished files go to Download/Gale, Movies/Gale, Music/Gale and Pictures/Gale.
  • If you sign in to Instagram (only needed for stories), the session cookies are kept in the app's private storage and used only to download what you ask for. Sign out in Settings removes them.
  • The built-in browser keeps cookies and site data like any browser, on the phone only. When it hands a download to Gale, the site's cookies are read at the moment Gale connects and are not saved with the download. Settings → Browser → "Clear cookies and site data" removes them. Its ad blocker uses a list of domains bundled with the app; no browsing history is sent anywhere.
  • Network connections: the servers of the files and pages you download, torrent peers for torrents, and (GitHub version only) GitHub every few days to update yt-dlp.

Your control

  • Remove any download from the list at any time (right-click → Remove).
  • Turn off browser interception, cookies or the media button in the extension options.
  • Uninstalling Gale removes the program and its browser and startup registrations. Your downloaded files and your list in %APPDATA%\com.faizzyhon.gale are kept so you do not lose them; delete that folder to remove everything.

Children

Gale is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes
